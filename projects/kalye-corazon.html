Kalye Corazon

About the Project

Kalye Corazon is an immersive pixel art game that takes players through the vibrant streets of Iloilo. Experience the rich culture and heritage of the city through this unique gaming adventure.

Features

  • Pixel art visuals inspired by Iloilo's architecture
  • Story-driven gameplay
  • Local cultural elements integration
  • Mini-games based on local traditions

Technologies Used

Godot GD Script Aseprite Figma Krita
